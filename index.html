ABOUT MISSIONQUANT

Experienced thinking.
Modern capability.

MissionQuant Solutions is a new professional services company, now in formation. It is being built around its founder's more than two decades of individual experience in government-contracting pricing and financial management.

The vision is to combine sound financial judgment, program discipline and AI-enabled technology to help organizations make better decisions and deliver outcomes efficiently.

As a company in formation, MissionQuant does not yet hold government registrations, certifications or contract awards, and it has no corporate past performance. The founder's experience is individual experience, not company past performance. We will state registrations, certifications and past performance here only once they are verified.
